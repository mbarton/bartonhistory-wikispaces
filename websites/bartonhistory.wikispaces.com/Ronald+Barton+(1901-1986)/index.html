Ronald Barton (1901-1986)

  • DBHB's grandfather.
  • Born 10 January 1901, middle son of Rev. Cecil Barton (1870-1909) and Esther Broadbent (1873-1959).
  • Full name 'Ronald Cecil Nicholson Barton'.
  • Contemporary of Clark Gable (1901-1960) and Louis Armstrong (1901-1971).
  • Married (on 7 June 1930 in Camberwell, London) Mary Carty Farrell (1907-1999).
  • In the 1920s he was an actor, mostly at the Old Vic (using stage name of 'Ronald Nicholson'). He once worked with Laurence Olivier.
  • Became a copy-writer at advertising firm S. H. Benson Ltd, and worked on famous Guinness adverts (see here and here).
  • His war-time diary covering eight months in 1941 is reproduced here.
  • After the war he wrote a radio adaptation of G. K. Chesterton's "The Man Who Was Thursday" for the BBC which was (re-?)broadcast in 1953 and 1975.
  • Father of Nicholas J. Barton.
  • Died 1986.
