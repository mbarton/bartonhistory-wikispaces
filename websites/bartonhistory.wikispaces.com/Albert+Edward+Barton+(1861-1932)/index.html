  • Son of Edward Barton (1828-1913) and Mary Anne Carter (1831-1911)
  • Married firstly Margaret.
  • Moved to America.
  • Married secondly Marguerite Norma Smith (1884-1979).

I'm indebted to Richard Hargreaves, a grandson of Albert by his first wife, for sharing his information on this branch with me, as well as Martin Barton and Paul Betteley.


Children


With Margaret:
  1. Robert Barton (1893-1916)
  2. Mary Barton (b.1895), who in 1915 married James Hargreaves.
  3. Margaret Davies Barton (b.1899), who in 1919 married Thomas Tuckniss Morris
  4. Bessie Barton, who married ???? Watson.

With Marguerite Norma Smith (1884-1979):
  • Six more children forming the American branch of this family. Some still living, so details not given.

With Edith Mary Nelson (a nurse who cared for Albert's children):
  • two more children.

With Rachel Battistolo (a Russian Bolshoi Ballet dancer on tour in New York in 1922):
  • a son, still living.
