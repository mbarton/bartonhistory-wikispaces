Emily Elliott (1839-1924)

  • DBHB's great x2 grandmother. Descendant of William the Conqueror, through her Boileau (Huguenot) heritage
  • Born 7 May 1839, daughter of Rev. Charles Boileau Elliott (1803-1875) and Emily Dougan (1806-1877).
  • Full name 'Emily Eugenia Elliott'. Known to some relatives as 'Millie'.
  • Contemporary of John Butler Yeats (1839-1922) and Thomas Hardy (1840-1928).
  • Married (on 12 October 1863, in Ludhiana, India), Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908).
  • Mother of Rev. Cecil Barton (1870-1909).
  • Died 11 February 1924, aged 84.

Emily described her own life in some detail in a really rather interesting memoir.
